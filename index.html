↗

My name is Shanmukh Aravapalli, and I’m majoring in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I chose this major because I love creating apps and training models that can help other people. I’m really interested in tech startups, and I have my own startup too. I’ve also done a machine learning internship. I’m taking CS180 to understand AI beyond just using its tools. I hope to learn how to check its work and use it responsibly when I’m building apps.

I mostly look at AI as something that helps me brainstorm and build on the skills I already have. It’s helped me come up with tech ideas for social problems, and I’m trying to bring those ideas to life. But I’ve also seen it make mistakes. Once, I asked AI to change one thing in my app, and it changed a lot of other code too. I had to go through the code to fix it. If I didn’t know how to code, that would have been much harder. That experience made me concerned about relying on AI without understanding what it’s doing. I’m most curious about the generative AI course outcome because I already use AI for brainstorming and coding, and I want to understand what it can do and where it can go wrong.

Outside class, I love to vibe code and cook different cuisines. I also paint and draw in my free time. I used to work as a VFX artist and worked on a couple of movies. I enjoy making things, whether that’s an app, a painting, or something new to eat.
